Button concept 08

Misregistered blue

Three lamps (sky, violet and periwinkle) cast the button's shadow. In register they multiply into one cobalt #2340E0 shadow. Hover to split them; click to merge them back.

Hover: the button lifts and its single cobalt shadow splits into sky, violet and periwinkle, staggered over about half a second.

Click: the button presses down and the three shadows snap back into one blue shadow, staying merged until the pointer leaves.
