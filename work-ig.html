IG

New trading web & mobile app

Brief

IG offers options, futures, CFDs and spread bets to retail clients in Europe, US and Asia. Its apps were 8-10 years old, with UX and technical debt and diverging web and mobile experiences.

User insights

Needs vary by asset class, strategy and device setup; a trader may use four or more screens. We narrowed scope to the top 10% of clients by value, who drove over 80% of revenue.

Process

I led the team gathering qual and quant, using modes of use, and day-in-the-life frameworks to structure complex user needs. Led six ideation design sprints.

IG

Web app

Project

A two-year programme of work, leading eight designers and 30 developers. Regular exec updates. The product was carefully released in a series of alphas & betas for rapid client feedback.

Outcome
  • Eliminated 1,000s of client support calls generated from legacy UX debt.
  • New platform was easier to onboard, prospect-to-client conversion time reduced by 15%.
  • In independent industry surveys traders rated IG's platforms as #1.
  • We aimed to minimise churn, so gave clients a free choice to switch. Within 6 months 70% of high value clients had done so.
IG

Mobile app redesign

Insight

Web took the lion's share of viewing time, yet 50% of trades were placed on mobile by high value clients logging in many times a day.

Process

I set up a dedicated qual and quant research team, then iteratively prototyped and tested concepts to create a new IA balancing IG-wide patterns with native Android and iOS design.

Outcome

Launched to a 4.6/5 App Store rating, the highest in its category at the time.
